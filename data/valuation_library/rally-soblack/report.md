## Methodological Limitations

The current engine applies neutral multipliers for time, market movement, condition, rarity, provenance, and liquidity. It therefore does not yet adjust older sales for changes in the Hermès resale market or differences in subject condition.

The 2022 Hong Kong sale was converted using a configured static FX rate rather than a documented historical spot rate for the sale date.

Future methodology versions should incorporate dated FX rates, buyer-premium normalization, condition adjustments, handbag-market indexes, and Rally-specific liquidity analysis.
