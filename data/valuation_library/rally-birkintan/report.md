## Interpretation

The valuation engine is effectively saying two things at once.

First, the broader completed-sale evidence for Ostrich Birkin 30 bags supports a market that often clears in the **low-$20,000s through high-$30,000s**.

Second, the model does **not yet have an exact completed Tangerine sale** to anchor the subject. The exact Tangerine examples located during research were useful identity and market-context observations, but they were not admitted as realized-price comparables.

That makes the **$21,974 base value mechanically valid under the current engine**, but it should not be interpreted as a precise appraisal of the subject. The unusually heavy 39.3% weight on the worn $13,970 Violine comparable is especially important. A future version of the methodology may want to handle known materially inferior condition more explicitly rather than relying on the existing similarity score alone.

## Key Risks and Limitations

The model is provisional and requires further methodological review. The most important limitations are:

- No current subject condition report.
- No completed exact-Tangerine realized-price comparable in the official set.
- Large dispersion across the six eligible comparables.
- Static configured FX rates are used for EUR-denominated sales rather than sale-date FX.
- Buyer-premium treatment is not fully normalized.
- The most heavily weighted comparable is materially worn, which may bias the base estimate downward if the Rally subject is in materially better condition.
