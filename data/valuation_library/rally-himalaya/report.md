## Interpretation

The strongest recent evidence clusters around **$85,000 to $97,536**, and the model's base value of **$93,750** sits almost exactly on Rally's latest observed market value of **$93,800**.

That means the current model does **not** identify a meaningful valuation gap between Rally's market price and its own base fair-value estimate.

The much higher 2015 comparable demonstrates that Himalaya pricing has historically reached substantially higher levels, but the current engine gives older observations limited weight and applies no explicit market-cycle adjustment.
